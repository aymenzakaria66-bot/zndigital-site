Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most often, and our answers.

Does the demo commit me to anything?

No. If you don't like the demo, you pay nothing.

How long does it take to get my website?

It depends on your request. We'll give you an estimate as soon as we first talk.

What is a domain name?

It's your website's address, for example yourbusiness.com. It's included for the first year and registered in your name.

Will my website look good on a phone?

Yes. All our websites automatically adapt to phones, tablets and computers.

Can I edit my website myself?

Yes, with the Complete package. Otherwise, we make the changes for you.

Does the website belong to me?

Yes. The domain is in your name and your online presence belongs to you.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Absolutely. We can modernize your current website and keep your domain name.
